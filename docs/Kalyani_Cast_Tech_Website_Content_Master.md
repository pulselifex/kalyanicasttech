### Who we are

Kalyani Cast Tech Limited (CIN: L26990DL2012PLC242760) is a BSE SME-listed engineering and manufacturing company (Scrip Code 544023). We manufacture standard and special-application freight containers, cast and machine ferrous components for railways and core industries, and are developing rail-linked cargo infrastructure in Kachchh, Gujarat.

### How the business developed

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| 2012 — Ferrous foundry and machine shop at Rewari | Incorporated in September 2012 with a Class A foundry to IS 12117:1996 and a machine shop at Village Mamaria Thethar, District Rewari, Haryana, supplying steel, SG iron and cast iron components to Indian Railways, cement, mining, chemical, fertiliser and power plants. |
| 2014 — First plant trial run | The first trial run at the Rewari works commenced in January 2014. |
| 2018 — First commercial double-stack dwarf run | A double-stack dwarf container service was commercially operated in July 2018, booked from Kanalus to Rewari. The dwarf container and its FRP flooring were developed in-house. |
| 2023 — BSE SME listing | Listed on the BSE SME platform in November 2023. |
| 2024 onwards — Shivlakha platform | Development of the Shivlakha rail and logistics platform began in Taluka Bhachau, District Kachchh. Container manufacturing and the Gati Shakti Cargo Terminal are operational; wagon manufacturing and the inland container depot are under development. |

> Each date traces to a company record, a dated press report or the BSE listing record. No range is asserted without a supporting source.

### What we focus on

Three activities make up our work:

1. Freight containers: standard 20' and 40' ISO containers, 40' and 42' dwarf containers, heavy open-top coiltainers, 20' tank and hopper containers, and CUBOID parcel and vehicle boxes.
2. Foundry and railway castings: container corner castings to ISO 1161 and modified 242 x 115 x 178 mm dwarf fittings, and machined railway castings from 20 kg to 2,000 kg.
3. The Shivlakha rail and logistics platform: connecting manufacturing with rail cargo handling in the Kachchh industrial belt.

We keep the status of each activity clear so customers can distinguish an established product or operational terminal from a facility still under development.

**H1.** Container & Foundry Manufacturing

**Hero.** *In-house corner castings, automated panel welding and full-scale structural load testing.*

### Container fabrication line

Our container lines at Rewari, Haryana and Shivlakha, Kachchh fabricate standard ISO containers and custom cargo boxes.

### Scope of the fabrication line

- Welding and assembly: automatic welding machines for side-wall, end-wall and roof panels; MIG welding machines operated by certified welders; dedicated jigs and fixtures for side walls, end walls, base underframes, doors and full container boxing.
- In-house components: standard (ISO 1161) and wide-body dwarf (242 x 115 x 178 mm) corner fittings, plus variable-thickness FRP composite flooring sheets.
- Surface treatment: 1,000 kg shot and sand blasting equipment and covered paint booths for primer and top coat.
- Testing: a full-scale container test rig to ISO 1496 Series-1, floor-strength test equipment for fork-lift wheel loads, and water-tightness testing.

### Class A foundry and machine shop, Rewari

Our Rewari foundry meets IS 12117:1996 (reaffirmed 2007) Class A Foundry requirements and pours single castings from 20 kg to 2,000 kg.

### Foundry scope

- Melting and sand plant: 2,000 kg and 1,000 kg induction furnaces; no-bake moulding with a 10 TPH continuous sand mixer, compaction and jolt moulding machines, and automatic sand reclamation covering knock-out, screening, fluidised bed cooling and drying.
- Heat treatment and finishing: 10-tonne heat-treatment furnace with automatic temperature control, recorders and water quenching; 1.5-tonne hanger shot-blasting machines, swing-frame grinders, pneumatic chippers and air-arc gouging.
- Machining: lathes, vertical borers, drilling and milling machines for supplying finished machined components.

### Location and regional context

The platform is located at Khasra No. 438, Village Shivlakha, Taluka Bhachau, District Kachchh, Gujarat 370145. It is being developed to connect industrial cargo in the Kachchh region with rail-linked transport, alongside our established container manufacturing plant at Rewari, Haryana.

The catchment is one of India's densest concentrations of bulk and process industry: industrial salt and marine chemicals, ceramics and wall tiles, steel and ferro-alloys, cement, fertiliser, chemicals and mineral exports. These cargo classes move in volume at low value density and are sensitive to handling cost, which is the profile that rail linkage addresses.

### Component status, separated by readiness

Each component is at a different stage. Operational infrastructure does not by itself confirm that a service is commercially available to a given cargo stream.

| Component | Current status | What this means for customers |
|---|---|---|
| Container Manufacturing | Operational | Container fabrication is active at Shivlakha. |
| Gati Shakti Cargo Terminal (GCT) | Operational | Rail-linked terminal infrastructure is operational. Specific customer movements depend on commodity permissions, handling requirements, route availability and commercial agreement. |
| Railway Wagon Manufacturing | Under Development — Target Q4 FY 2026-27 | Rolling-stock fabrication is under development and not yet taking commercial production orders. |
| Inland Container Depot (ICD) | Under Development — Target Q1 FY 2027-28 | The inland depot is under development. Customs and depot services open only after regulatory commissioning. |

### Cargo streams we can evaluate

We evaluate bulk minerals, industrial salt, steel and metals, ceramics, general industrial cargo, and containerised export-import flows moving into or out of the Kachchh industrial region.

### How we assess a cargo opportunity

Rail is not automatically cheaper or better than road for every movement. Four stages stand between an enquiry and a proposal.

| Stage | Caption |
|---|---|
| Cargo and volume | The commodity, its packaging, shipment volume and frequency. |
| Route and mode | Origin, destination, current road or rail mode, and wagon compatibility. |
| First mile, terminal and last mile | Road distance between the factory or warehouse and the terminal, loading and unloading equipment, and any storage need. |
| Total logistics cost and trial feasibility | We compare the full door-to-door chain before proposing a rail movement or a trial. Movements that do not hold up are identified here and reported as such. |

### What an enquiry does and does not do

Submitting cargo details starts a technical and commercial review. It does not confirm a route, a tariff, a service level or an operating start date.
