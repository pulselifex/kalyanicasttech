## 1. Statutory Ground Rule on Capacity Disclosures

Per Section 7 of the Master Brief (30 October 2026):
1. **Never Combine Capacities:** The capabilities of Rewari Works (Parent: Kalyani Cast Tech Limited) and Shivlakha Platform (Wholly Owned Subsidiary: KMT Engineering Private Limited) must never be aggregated into a single combined figure.
2. **Purge Unverified 6,000 Containers/Year Claim:** The legacy unverified claim that Rewari produces "6,000 containers/year" has been completely removed across all content files, component copy, and asset metadata.
3. **Statutory Baseline Attribution:** Rewari container capacity must be cited solely against official **IRS Capacity Report DLH21X014** (baseline ≥1,000 ISO freight containers/year for 20' HC 34T scope).
4. **Shivlakha Sole Expansion Repository:** Shivlakha is the sole repository for the 10,000 stated capacity and 25,000 container expansion target by Q4 FY 2026–27.

## 2. Facility 1: Rewari Manufacturing Works (Haryana)

- **Operating Entity:** Kalyani Cast Tech Limited (Listed Parent Company)
- **Location:** Village Mamaria Thethar, District Rewari, Haryana – 123101, India (approx. 90 km from New Delhi Airport)
- **Footprint:** 5 acres industrial site
- **Covered Workshop Area:** >6,000 sq.m across 4 specialized fabrication, assembly, and blast/paint sheds
- **Foundry Scope:** IS 12117:1996 Class A ferrous foundry; 20 kg to 2,000 kg single pours (cast steel, ductile SG iron, alloy iron)
- **Corner Castings Approval:** Lloyd's Register Approved Foundry (Certificate: LR2489004WA, valid to 30 June 2027)
- **Container Capacity Baseline:** Assessed by Indian Register of Shipping (IRS) under Report DLH21X014 with approved baseline capacity ≥1,000 ISO containers/year (20ft High Cube 34T)
- **In-House Validation Equipment:** ISO 1496 Series-1 structural test rig (corner-post stacking to 96,012 kg, racking, floor load tester up to 7,260 kg wheel load, water spray testing curtain, optical emission spectrometer, Charpy sub-zero impact bath).

## 3. Facility 2: Shivlakha Integrated Rail & Logistics Platform (Gujarat)

- **Operating Entity:** KMT Engineering Private Limited (Wholly Owned Subsidiary SPV)
- **Location:** Khasra No. 438, Village Shivlakha, Taluka Bhachau, District Kachchh, Gujarat – 370145, India
- **Footprint:** 114 acres (233,304 sq.m NA-approved master layout)
- **Direct Connectivity:** Adjacent to National Highway NH-27; connected via private rail siding directly to Indian Railways Mundra–Kandla–Palanpur broad-gauge mainline (7.33 km from Chitrod Junction)
- **Operational Infrastructure:**
  - **Container Manufacturing:** Stated operational capacity of 10,000 containers/year, with ongoing planned expansion to 25,000 containers/year by Q4 FY 2026–27.
  - **Gati Shakti Cargo Terminal (GCT):** 720 m × 30 m broad-gauge private rail siding (CC+8 axle load rating). Western Railway Terminal Alpha Code: `KEGS`, Numerical Code: `08524078`. Inaugurated 15 September 2026.
- **Under-Development Infrastructure:**
  - **Railway Wagon Manufacturing:** Licensed for ~2,400 wagons/annum. RDSO Stage-I concept approval secured 26 October 2026. Target commissioning: Q4 FY 2026–27.
  - **Inland Container Depot (ICD):** 30-acre customs-bonded enclosure backed by formal CBIC Letter of Intent (10 September 2026). Target operational date: Q1 FY 2027–28.
