## 1. Objective & Purpose
This register catalogs items requiring formal corporate, secretarial, technical, or statutory confirmation from Kalyani Cast Tech Limited before final public sign-off or external publication. In accordance with progressive disclosure and statutory integrity guidelines, no information has been fabricated.

## 2. Open Verification & Content Approval Items

### Item AR-01: Promoter Identity Verification — Jay Bhagnani vs. Jai Prakash Bangani
- **Category:** Statutory & Secretarial Governance
- **Current Presentation:** Listed under *Promoter Group – Experience & Strategic Contribution* as **"Mr. Jay Bhagnani"**, Promoter Group & Strategic Advisor.
- **Copy:** *"An entrepreneur based in Kolkata with experience in running an NBFC, Jay Bhagnani advises the Company on strategic planning and financial matters."*
- **Flagged Discrepancy:** Earlier BSE SME disclosures, prospectus filings, and board registers recorded the promoter as **"Jai Prakash Bangani"**.
- **Action Required:** Company Secretary (CS) to confirm whether a formal deed poll, gazette notification, or updated DIN record exists for "Jay Bhagnani", or whether statutory filings require "Jai Prakash Bangani" (or "Jay Bhagnani alias Jai Prakash Bangani").
- **Current UI Guardrail:** A discreet statutory note is displayed beneath the profile acknowledging the earlier corporate records.

### Item AR-02: Shivlakha Container Manufacturing Capacity
- **Category:** Technical / Operational Evidence
- **Current Presentation:** Stated current container manufacturing capacity of **10,000 containers per year**.
- **Planned Expansion:** Expansion target of **25,000 containers per year by Q4 FY 2026–27**.
- **Required Approval:**
  1. Technical director or plant head to provide the classification society assessment (e.g., IRS capacity certificate) or internal engineering calculation validating whether 10,000 units represents installed, rated, or single-shift tooling capacity.
  2. Board approval or capital expenditure disclosure confirming the planned expansion timeline to 25,000 units.
- **Current UI Guardrail:** Text explicitly qualifies: *"Shivlakha currently has a stated container manufacturing capacity of 10,000 containers per year, with planned expansion to 25,000 containers per year by Q4 FY 2026–27."* It does not claim 25,000 units as commissioned capacity.

### Item AR-03: GCT Inauguration Verification (15 September 2026)
- **Category:** Event & Public Relations Record
- **Current Presentation:** Inaugurated on 15 September 2026 by Hon'ble Minister of Railways Shri Ashwini Vaishnaw.
- **Required Confirmation:** Western Railway press release or internal dispatch log confirming the exact ministerial inauguration date of 15 September 2026 for the first commercial freight train dispatch from siding code KEGS (08524078).
- **Current UI Guardrail:** Terminal status is marked *Operational*, but descriptions clarify that specific commercial cargo movements are evaluated on route and commodity feasibility.

### Item AR-04: Inland Container Depot (ICD) CBIC Letter of Intent & IMC Review
- **Category:** Customs & Regulatory Documentation
- **Current Presentation:** LoI issued following Inter-Ministerial Committee (IMC) review on 25 August 2026 for the proposed 30-acre ICD. Management target Q1 FY 2027–28.
- **Omitted Details per Brief:** Statutory 1-year operationalisation window, NA-approved layout, and 114-acre/233,304 sq.m footprint have been intentionally omitted from public card displays to avoid over-promising or customer confusion.
- **Required Confirmation:** Copy of the formal LoI from the Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC) for corporate records prior to commercial marketing of EXIM customs services.

### Item AR-05: Railway Wagon Manufacturing Status & Timeline
- **Category:** Rolling Stock & RDSO Approvals
- **Current Presentation:** Facility under development with management target of **Q4 FY 2026–27**. Concept design approved by RDSO (Stage-I on 26 Oct 2026).
- **Required Confirmation:** Production schedule and RDSO Stage-II prototype manufacturing sanction before claiming commercial availability of wagons.

### Item AR-06: Publication Rights for Visual Assets
- **Category:** Legal & Intellectual Property
- **Items:** Drone photography, executive portraits, and satellite terminal imagery hosted in `/assets/` and `/public/images/`.
- **Required Confirmation:** Written confirmation from management that all displayed industrial site photos are authorized for public investor and commercial viewing.
