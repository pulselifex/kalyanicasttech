## 2. Risk Mitigation & Forward-Looking Statement Controls

1. **No Guaranteed Commercial Outcomes**: The Rake and Payload Calculator incorporates clear explanatory disclaimers noting that payload optimizations are indicative engineering estimates based on permissible axle loads and wagon tare weights, subject to Indian Railways commercial rules.
2. **Statutory Integrity**: All financial statements, shareholding patterns, and corporate governance disclosures refer directly to official filings submitted to BSE Limited under SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.
3. **No Unapproved Endorsements**: Concept renderings and engineering illustrations are clearly separated from authentic factory and terminal photographs.
