## 2. Testing Capability vs. Product Certification Distinction

In compliance with corporate governance policies, the website enforces a clear distinction:
1. **In-House Testing Capability:** Facilities such as the ISO 1496 testing rig, 40T UTM, -45°C Charpy impact machine, and optical emission spectrometer are described as internal verification and quality assurance tools.
2. **Third-Party Product Type Approvals:** Only formal external certificates (Lloyd's Register EMEA `LR2489004WA` and IRS `DLH21X014`) are cited as product approvals.
3. **Rolling Stock Clearance:** RDSO Stage-I is presented strictly as a concept validation stage, ensuring no prospective customer assumes commercial availability.
