The refined master is factually careful and editorially clean. It is also commercially inert.

What is strong

Factual discipline is genuinely good

Across 4,677 words there is not one invented specification, capacity, approval, customer, saving or operating date. That is rare and it is the right foundation. Nothing in this review recommends relaxing it.

The status discipline holds

Shivlakha components are kept separate, the ICD is described as under development throughout, and product concepts are not presented as live products. This survives review by a technical or compliance reader.

Internal notes are correctly separated

Implementation guidance, verification notes and public copy are cleanly divided. A developer can work from this without pasting internal text live.

Voice is consistent and professional

Direct company voice throughout, no superlatives, no filler vocabulary, no unsupported promotional claims.

Metadata is unique and controlled

34 unique titles, meta descriptions and URLs, with a completed duplicate audit.

What remains weak

Zero specificity — the central commercial failure

The copy contains no dimension, no weight, no capacity, no area, no distance and no date. A procurement engineer looking for a 20-foot high-cube payload cannot find it. A cargo owner cannot find out how far Shivlakha is from a port. A specification buyer cannot tell whether this company builds 20-foot boxes or 40-foot boxes. Every claim has been hedged into abstraction, and abstraction does not convert in industrial B2B.

Over-correction in the previous pass

In protecting against unverified claims, the refinement removed the concrete anchors that make a manufacturer credible. The result is a site that cannot be faulted on accuracy and cannot be used to make a buying decision. That trade was made too far in one direction.

31 of 34 pages are too thin to rank or to persuade

Average length is 138 words. Thin pages neither satisfy a search query nor give a technical reader anything to act on. The thinnest product pages — Container manufacturing at 136 words, Design and engineering at 121, Product data sheets at 79 — are the pages most likely to be read by a serious buyer.

The buyer journey is described but not built

The copy explains that a container should be selected around the cargo, then does not provide the structure that lets a visitor do so. There is no application-to-product mapping, no parameter list, no comparison framework, no intake checklist. The journey is asserted in prose rather than implemented as page structure.

Investor Relations is a set of empty signposts

Eight pages averaging 66 words. There is no document register, no period column, no filing index, no link structure. A listed company's investor section that says 'documents are provided according to official issue dates' without listing one document is not useful to an investor or a regulator-facing reviewer.

Two pairs of pages duplicate each other

Testing and quality overlaps Quality and certifications at 0.36 similarity; Technical data sheets overlaps Product data sheets at 0.33. Both pairs would compete for the same query and confuse the same visitor.

Calls to action are labels, not actions

CTAs such as 'Explore Container Products' and 'View Enquiry Fields' name a destination without a verifiable process behind it. There is no routing, no acknowledgement behaviour and no measurement event defined.

No visual evidence plan

The copy refers to photography in passing. There is no shot list, no authenticity control and no statement of what a visitor is entitled to see. A manufacturer with a real plant and real products is not showing either.

Why this is a commercial problem, not an editorial one

The gap is not editorial polish. It is that the site currently gives a buyer no way to judge relevance. A container manufacturer whose website states no container parameter is not credible to a technical buyer, however well written the surrounding sentences are.

What to do

  1. Do not rewrite the structure or the voice. Both are sound.
  2. Close the figure reconciliation, then reinstate approved specifics under a status label on the pages that need them.
  3. Build the buyer journey into page structure: application mapping, parameter registers, intake checklists, comparison frameworks.
  4. Rebuild Investor Relations as a document register rather than a set of descriptive pages.
  5. Merge the two overlapping page pairs and redirect.
  6. Replace CTA labels with defined actions, destinations and measurement events.
  7. Commission a real-asset shot list before launch. The plant and the products are the strongest evidence available and neither currently appears.
Honesty note

This review inspected the refined master, the underlying source documents supplied for this project, and the live website. Where a figure is proposed for reinstatement, it is drawn from a company-supplied document and is marked as requiring owner approval. No figure has been verified against an audited or statutory source in this pass, and none is presented as verified.

Final decision

Major commercial revision required

This is not a decision about writing quality. The refined master is well written, factually careful and structurally sound. It is not publishable because it does not do the job a manufacturer’s website has to do.

Three findings drive the decision. First, the site contains no figure of any kind across 4,677 words — no dimension, no weight, no capacity, no area, no distance and no date. A technical buyer cannot assess a product, a procurement evaluator cannot compare a facility and a cargo owner cannot judge a route. Second, nine critical red-team findings are open, including an unresolved registered-office conflict, an unconfirmed terminal commissioning status and eight Investor Relations pages with no document behind them. Third, the site has no approved imagery, so a real plant and real products are invisible while the visitor is asked to take capability on trust.

What should happen next is specific. Close the facility-figure reconciliation and the product master, then reinstate approved specifics under status labels on the twelve pages identified in Section 02. Merge the four overlapping page pairs. Rebuild Investor Relations as a register. Implement the CTA destinations and the eight measurement events. Commission the shot list. None of this requires relaxing the factual discipline the previous pass established — it requires completing it, because a qualification with no fact behind it protects nobody.
