Kalyani Cast Tech Limited (CIN: L26990DL2012PLC242760) is a BSE SME-listed engineering and manufacturing company. We design and manufacture freight containers and develop specialised cargo equipment for industrial, rail and export-import movements.

Our work centres on understanding what the cargo needs, developing equipment that suits it, and assessing how manufacturing and infrastructure can support real freight movements.

We built our container manufacturing capability at Rewari, Haryana, and are extending that foundation into a broader rail and logistics platform at Shivlakha, Kachchh, Gujarat. A workable cargo solution starts with the application. We look at the cargo, its dimensions, how it is loaded and unloaded, handling constraints, the route, movement frequency and any operating requirement. Only then do we confirm which configuration fits.

**Hero:** _Container configurations built around what you are moving._

## Standard freight containers

General-purpose and high-cube formats cover the majority of dry freight movements. Selection depends on cargo dimensions, total weight, loading method, handling equipment, transport mode and operating conditions. Nominal size alone does not confirm suitability.

For each approved model we provide applicable dimensions, tare weight, payload, maximum gross weight, construction details, access arrangement and relevant standards from the current controlled data sheet.

## Specialised containers

Some cargo needs a configuration beyond a general-purpose container. Our material covers configurations such as dwarf and wider-dwarf containers, open-top, tank, hopper, stainless-steel and other special-cargo designs.

The current status and approved technical scope of each configuration is confirmed before it is presented as an available product. If a concept is historical or still under development, we state that rather than presenting it as available.

## Rail, parcel and vehicle cargo

Certain configurations are designed around parcel, two-wheeler, three-wheeler and similar application-specific cargo. Suitability depends on the cargo itself, the equipment design, the loading process and the wagon or operating conditions that apply. Compatibility and operating permissions are checked for the specific configuration and intended movement.

## Application-specific designs

When a cargo needs a different access arrangement, internal layout, material, loading method or handling interface, we assess the use case against our existing approved models or evaluate whether further technical development is required. A development or manufacturing commitment follows only after feasibility and scope are agreed.

## How to select

Send the cargo description, dimensions, total weight, loading method, expected movement and any equipment or route constraint. We will identify the relevant model information and tell you whether further technical review is needed.

## The platform

At Village Shivlakha, Taluka Bhachau, District Kachchh, Gujarat, we are developing a rail and logistics platform covering container manufacturing, a Gati Shakti Cargo Terminal, railway wagon manufacturing and an inland container depot.

The intent is to give cargo moving into and out of the Kachchh industrial region a rail-linked option that is worth evaluating.

## Component status

Container manufacturing at Shivlakha is operational. The Gati Shakti Cargo Terminal is operational. Wagon manufacturing is under development, with a target readiness in the fourth quarter of FY 2026-27. The inland container depot is under development, with a target operational date in the first quarter of FY 2027-28.

These are separate components at different stages of readiness. Their operating status, service scope and milestones must be understood individually. We do not present a planned or developing service as available before it is confirmed.

## Cargo we can evaluate

Bulk minerals, salt, steel and metals, ceramics, industrial cargo and containerised export-import flows. Whether a particular movement suits a rail-linked arrangement depends on the cargo, the route, the volume and the handling.

## How to start

Share the cargo type, origin and destination, approximate volume, current mode and route, loading arrangement and any handling constraint. We will identify what has to be verified before a route or operating proposal can be considered.
