Scope and method

How this document works

The source document was treated as the working foundation, not replaced. Its structure, positioning and information architecture are preserved. What changed is the language, the commercial relevance, the SEO metadata and the separation of public copy from internal control.

Positioning preserved

Engineering Solutions for Cargo Movement, with the supporting line unchanged. No evidence-based reason was found to alter the approved direction.

Structure preserved

Eight navigation areas and every page in the source inventory. No page removed. No new page created.

Facts preserved

No specification, figure, date, approval or capability was added. Statements that could not be supported were qualified, not strengthened.

The governing rule applied throughout

Improve the language, strengthen the commercial relevance, preserve the facts and never invent capability. Where the source copy could be read as implying something the company cannot yet support — an available product, an operating facility, a route, a saving, a commitment — the wording was tightened rather than left ambiguous. Those decisions are recorded in the editorial change log (Section 2) and the factual verification register (Section 3).

What this document is

A refined, publication-ready content master covering every page and subpage in the source document, with metadata, calls to action, internal links and implementation guidance. It is ready to hand to a web developer once the verification register is closed.

What it is not

It is not publication-approved. Product specifications, facility figures, project statuses, statutory contact details and every Investor Relations page require the approvals listed in Section 3. Nothing here authorises a factual claim that the source document did not support.

Two claims deliberately not strengthened

Two areas in the source material were at the greatest risk of being read as something the company cannot yet support, and both were rewritten rather than polished.

Product availability. The source material references configurations such as dwarf, wider-dwarf, open-top, tank, hopper, stainless-steel, side-access, coiltainer and Cuboid or LTL designs. Those references are real and were retained, but every one now carries a status position — current, historical, developmental or awaiting confirmation — because a historical brochure is not evidence of a live product. Publishing the catalogue without that distinction would be the single largest factual exposure on the website.

Shivlakha status. The platform is not one facility at one status. Container and wagon manufacturing, the Gati Shakti Cargo Terminal and the inland container depot are separate components at different stages. The refined copy states the status of each component individually, avoids any implication of a single operational end-to-end service, and keeps the inland container depot described as under development.

How to read the page copy

Each page is presented as a title block followed by the public copy. The title block is a specification, not copy: it carries the proposed URL, SEO title, meta description and H1 for implementation. The Hero line and the headed blocks below it are the copy that goes on the page. Calls to action, internal links, the image direction and the internal verification note are implementation guidance.
