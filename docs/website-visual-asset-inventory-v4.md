## 3. Disallowed Images & Exclusions

1. **Unverified Certificate Stubs**: Any generic cards without scanned backing documents (e.g. unverified ISO 9001 / IS 12117 cards stating *"Certificate documentation is being updated"*) are excluded from public presentation and retained solely in the internal audit register.
2. **CAD General Arrangement Drawings as Primary Product Cards**: CAD line drawings remain excluded from primary catalog cards (reserved strictly for slide-over engineering specification drawers).
3. **Redundant Duplicate Images**: Multiple instances of `Spl Cont Salt n Steel cargoHD.jpeg` across standard container cards remain banned.
4. **Internal Working Documents**: `Kalyani Cast Tech Limited — Master Content Document _ Website & Brochure Edition V1.0.pdf` is strictly excluded from public download links.
